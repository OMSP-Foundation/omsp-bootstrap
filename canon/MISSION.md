# OMSP Mission

## 1. Mission Statement

OMSP provides an open engineering foundation that enables interoperable maritime operational knowledge.

Its mission is to establish the canonical language, models, governance, and engineering practices required to create trustworthy, traceable, and reusable maritime systems knowledge.

## 2. Mission Objectives

OMSP advances this mission by:

- defining shared maritime operational terminology;
- creating model-based representations of operational knowledge;
- maintaining governance and engineering standards;
- preserving traceability across issues, artifacts, decisions, baselines, and releases;
- enabling downstream repositories to build on common foundations;
- supporting AI-assisted engineering within explicit human accountability boundaries.

## 3. Program Alignment

OMSP work should align to at least one of these mission outcomes:

- clearer shared language;
- stronger model-based understanding;
- improved interoperability;
- better governance and traceability;
- reusable engineering foundations;
- safer and more accountable automation support.

## 4. Out of Scope

The mission does not include unmanaged product deployment, proprietary lock-in, undocumented authority changes, or replacing domain expertise with unreviewed automation.

## 5. Downstream Use

Downstream artifacts may cite this Mission when explaining why a Work Package, architecture decision, standard, model, or repository exists.

Mission alignment should be visible in issue objectives, PR summaries, decision records, and baseline notes when material.
