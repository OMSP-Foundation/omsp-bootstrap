# Digital Twin Governance Review Notes

Review focus:

- authority separation between configuration, observation, state, and derived values;
- explicit accountable human command authority;
- stop-use and escalation behavior for uncertain or unsafe conditions;
- AI-assistance labeling, review, and prohibited actions;
- distinction between repository acceptance and operational authorization;
- independent review for safety-relevant changes;
- visibility of provenance, quality, freshness, conflicts, and limitations;
- absence of certification, seaworthiness, or vessel-control claims.

A successful review confirms governance completeness within the Sprint-4 reference scope only.
