# OMSP Vision

## 1. Vision Statement

OMSP exists to become the open, model-based, knowledge-first systems engineering foundation for maritime operations.

It aims to make maritime operational knowledge interoperable, traceable, reusable, and governable across people, systems, organizations, and future autonomous or AI-assisted workflows.

## 2. Long-Term Direction

OMSP envisions an engineering ecosystem where:

- maritime operational knowledge is captured as durable models, not scattered documents;
- architecture, terminology, governance, and implementation remain traceable to shared canon;
- open foundations enable trustworthy collaboration across repositories and organizations;
- systems engineering practices are accessible, inspectable, and reusable;
- AI-assisted development strengthens human-governed engineering rather than replacing accountability.

## 3. Foundation Role

The OMSP Foundation provides the canonical language, governance, engineering standards, and traceability rules that downstream repositories can reference.

Downstream artifacts should use this vision as the highest-level statement of intended direction when interpreting program scope, architectural choices, and engineering priorities.

## 4. Vision Boundaries

The OMSP vision does not imply unreviewed production readiness, unmanaged operational deployment, or replacement of accountable human engineering authority.

The vision is realized through governed Work Packages, reviewed artifacts, traceable decisions, and controlled baselines.

## 5. Reference Use

Downstream artifacts may cite this Vision when they need to justify:

- knowledge-first design;
- model-based engineering;
- maritime operational interoperability;
- open foundation behavior;
- traceable governance and engineering practices;
- human-accountable AI assistance.

## 6. Maintenance

This Vision is maintained through issue-backed Work Packages and reviewed pull requests. Material changes require governance review and version metadata update.
